# knowledge/business

Business context that shapes the product: company strategy, ICP/personas at the business
level, pricing, market/competition, moat, go-to-market.

## What lives here
- `svt-context.md` — extracted real Svayantra Tech context (`[REAL]`). **Start here.**
- Add: `pricing.md`, `competition.md`, `gtm.md`, `market-sizing.md` as they're researched.

## Rule
Anything sourced from a real internal SVT document is tagged `[REAL]`. Anything inferred or
assumed is tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` with a confidence note. Keep the two clearly separated so
future decisions know what's evidence vs hypothesis.
