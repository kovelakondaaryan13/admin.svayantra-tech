# knowledge/users

Who uses RevenueOS, what they're trying to get done, and what we've learned about them.

## What lives here
- `personas.md` — Founder, Head of Sales, Sales Rep, Operations (+ future: Clients, Admins,
  Managers). Grounded in `../business/svt-context.md` ICP.
- `jobs-to-be-done.md` — the jobs each persona hires RevenueOS for.
- `research-notes.md` — findings from user interviews/usage (PostHog) as they accumulate.

## Seeded personas (skeleton — expand from blueprint Part 1)
- **Founder** — wants visibility and to stop being the operating system; asks "why are deals
  slowing down?", "show pipeline health."
- **Head of Sales** — team throughput, pipeline health, forecasting, coaching.
- **Sales Rep** — "what should I work on?", prep for meetings, generate proposals fast.
- **Operations** — clean data, handoffs, quotations, follow-ups (AR).

## Rule
Distinguish `[REAL]` user evidence (interviews, analytics) from `[ASSUMPTION]`. Update after
real usage data arrives (PostHog) — assumptions get replaced by evidence over time.
